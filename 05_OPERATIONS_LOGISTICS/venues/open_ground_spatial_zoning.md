## 2. Dual-Zone Climate & HVAC Engineering

The expanded 16,000 sq ft pavilion is engineered with strict thermal segregation to maintain maximum guest comfort without wasting electrical power:

### Zone A: Ceremonial & Viewing AC Sanctuary (Mandap, Seating & Stage 1)
- **Floor Area:** ~8,500–9,000 sq ft (~55% of main pavilion).
- **Target Temperature:** **22°C to 24°C** continuously under full ambient and lighting load.
- **Cooling Equipment:** **60 to 80 Metric Tons** of ductable packaged outdoor tower AC units with overhead fabric diffuser socks concealed along the perimeter truss.
- **Enclosure Integrity:** Sealed with double-layer insulated velvet draught-curtains and heavy PVC magnetic flap doors at entry points.

### Zone C: Main Buffet, Dining Hall & Culinary Promenade
- **Floor Area:** ~4,500–5,000 sq ft (~30% of main pavilion).
- **Cooling Equipment:** **10 to 12 High-Velocity Industrial Evaporative Desert Coolers** (minimum 12,000 CFM each) positioned along perimeter vents.
- **Air-Circulation Strategy:** High-volume low-speed (HVLS) hanging ceiling fans circulate air toward active exhaust louvers, expelling cooking aromas and steam away from the guest areas.

### Private Dining Pod (30 ft / Adjacent VIP Tent)
- **Floor Area:** ~1,800–2,400 sq ft.
- **Cooling Equipment:** Dedicated **10–15 Ton independent packaged AC unit**, providing a quiet, low-noise, private chilled dining environment for immediate family, elders, and VIPs.

### Haldi Annex (Top-Left Attached Ritual Courtyard)
- **Design:** Dedicated semi-enclosed exterior courtyard / canopy.
- **Climate:** Natural cross-ventilation supplemented with heavy-duty mist fans and portable coolers. Segregates all water and turmeric stains completely from the main pavilion's carpeted interior.

### Thermal Separation Barrier:
- A heavy **acoustic and thermal insulated double-drape partition** (with air-curtain blowers over central passages) separates Zone A from Zone C, preventing kitchen humidity and heat from collapsing the AC pressure in the ceremonial sanctuary.

## 4. Back-of-House & Caterer Circulation

1. **Dedicated Service Corridor:** A 10-foot wide exterior sheltered corridor links the hotel base kitchens directly to the marquee service entry.
2. **Zero Guest Cross-Traffic:** Catering staff, soiled dishware trolleys, and live refill vessels move exclusively behind the eastern service partition.
3. **Power & Generator Isolation:** Caterer induction warmers and deep-fryer loads run on Bus C of the synchronized DG farm, completely isolated from Mandap audio and lighting circuits.

## 6. Luxury Mobile Sanitation & Drainage Infrastructure

To eliminate guest travel back to the hotel lobby for restrooms and prevent mud/sullage pooling on open ground:

1. **Luxury 4-Cabin Executive Washroom Trailer:**
   - Stationed discreetly 15 meters behind Zone C dining hall with private screened access path.
   - Features: 2 Male + 2 Female independent luxury cabins, continuous air-conditioning, porcelain fixtures, sensor taps, backlit LED vanity mirrors, automated scent diffusers, and luxury hand soaps.
   - Attendant: 1 dedicated janitorial staff assigned continuously throughout all functions for 100% spotless turnaround.
2. **Water Supply & Closed-Loop Drainage:**
   - **Fresh Water:** 2,000L trailer-mounted water bowser with booster pressure pump.
   - **Greywater / Blackwater:** Self-contained heavy-duty holding tank with vacuum pump-out truck on call post-event. Zero ground dumping.

## 7. Satellite Finishing Kitchen & Hot-Holding Logistics

To solve the 50-meter food transit degradation:

1. **On-Ground Satellite Finishing Hub (Zone C Rear):**
   - Equipped with dedicated gas tandoors (fresh rotis/naans), commercial deep-fryers (hot puris, pakoras, jalebis), and 4 mobile electric hot-holding proofer cabinets (maintaining gravies at 65°C+).
2. **BOH Screened Service Corridor:** Insulated hot-food Cambro carriers transit along the eastern perimeter alleyway. Zero guest sightline contact with dishwashing or trash disposal.
3. **Sullage Grease-Trap:** Dishwashing sink wastewater funnels through a portable 3-chamber grease-trap before discharge into municipal drainage.

## 8. Acoustic Engineering & 22:00 Municipal Curfew Management

1. **Line-Array Directional Targeting:** Sangeet sound speakers positioned facing inward toward the center dance floor with downward tilt, preventing high-frequency audio bleed through marquee sidewalls.
2. **Acoustic Kitchen Partition:** Double-layer heavy velvet drapes hung over the Zone A / Zone C dividing wall to absorb catering cutlery clatter during morning Vedic chanting.
3. **22:00 Curfew Protocol:** In compliance with local Rayagada SDPO regulations, external high-output subwoofers are dialed back at 22:00, switching to focused interior ambient sound for midnight Sangeet revelry.
