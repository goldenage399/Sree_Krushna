# 🎁 Gifts, Shagun & Exchange Registry

**SSOT Scope:** Tracking customary gift exchanges between bride & groom households, incoming guest shagun envelopes, and outbound return gifts (*Bidhai*).

---

## Gift Exchange Streams

1. **Family-to-Family Customary Exchanges:** Attire, jewelry, sweets, and silver articles presented during Nirbandha, Barat, and Chauthi.
2. **Guest Shagun & Envelopes:** Cataloging incoming cash gifts and items at the reception gift desk with assigned custodians: [`PER-010`](file:///d:/GitHub_Repo/Sree_Krushna/03_PEOPLE_GUESTS/directory/).
3. **Return Gifts (*Bidhai / Return Favors*):** Curated sweets boxes and silver coins distributed to attending families.
