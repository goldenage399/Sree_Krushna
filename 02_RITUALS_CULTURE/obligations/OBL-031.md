# `OBL-031` — Sadu Barana (ସାଢୁ ବରଣ - ଲଡୁ ଓ ପୋଷାକ)

## 1. Cultural Context & Significance
*Sadu Barana* (ସାଢୁ ବରଣ) is an authentic Odia wedding custom conducted at the Mandap. The Bride's elder sister's husband (*Sadu Bhai* / *Didi's Husband*) performs a ceremonial welcoming and grants customary permission (ଅନୁମତି) for the Groom to ascend the sacred Mandap. In recognition of this honor and role, the Groom's family presents the *Sadu Barana* offerings — an auspicious sweet hamper (Motichoor/Besan Laddoo box) and formal dress (Kurta/Shirt set) — to the Sadu Bhai.
