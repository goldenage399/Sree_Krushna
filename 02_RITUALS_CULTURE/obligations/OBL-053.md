# `OBL-053` — Ceremonial Mukuta & Hastaganthi Bandhana Vastra (ବିବାହ ମୁକୁଟ ଓ ହସ୍ତଗଣ୍ଠି ବନ୍ଧନ ବସ୍ତ୍ର)

## 1. Cultural Context & Significance
Sacred bridal & groom ceremonial Mukuta (Shola & silver filigree crowns) and nuptial knotting cloth for Hastaganthi binding. Grounded in Odia Hindu traditions for EVT-004 (RIT-005). Customarily procured and presented by the Bride's family as consecrated Mandap Samagri (`SAM-005`) for the coronation of the couple and the sacred Hastaganthi knotting rite.
