## 1. Overview of Odia Vedic Wedding Customs

Traditional Odia Hindu weddings are steeped in ancient Vedic injunctions combined with rich regional cultural expressions:
- **Deva Nimantrana:** First formal invitation offered to Lord Jagannath at Puri (or family deity) along with betel nut (*Gua*) and sacred cloth.
- **Nirbandha:** Formal Vedic promise of alliance between families with exchange of rings, gifts, and horoscope confirmation.
- **Mangan & Snana:** Traditional ritual bath with raw turmeric and fragrant oils early morning before the wedding.
- **Baranugam & Barat Reception:** Welcoming the groom with aarti, coconut, and sacred water.
- **Kanyadaan:** Sacred giving away of the bride by parents/Kanyadata.
- **Saptapadi & Lajahoma:** Seven sacred steps and puffed rice offerings to Agni Deva invoking lifelong companionship and dharma.
- **Sindoor Daan:** Application of sacred vermilion and Mangalsutra dharan.
- **Chauthi:** Sacred post-wedding rite on the fourth night marking the formal beginning of domestic life (*Grihastha Ashram*).
- **Astamangala:** Eighth-day visit to the bride's parental home.
