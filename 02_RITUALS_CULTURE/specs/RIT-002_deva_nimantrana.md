# 🕉️ RIT-002: Deva Nimantrana (First Card Offering to Lord Jagannath)

## 1. Liturgical Meaning & Significance
In Odia tradition, the very first wedding invitation card (*Patrika*) is consecrated and offered to the Supreme Lord — Lord Jagannath at Puri Jagannath Temple (or the family Kuladevata) — seeking divine sanction, protection, and boundless grace for the marriage.

## 2. Protocol & Tradition
- The printed invitation card is presented inside a sacred brass/silver thali along with whole betel nuts (*Gua*), betel leaves (*Pana*), sacred cloth (*Khandua / Pata*), flower garland, coconut, and customary offering (*Dakshina*).
- Prayers are offered by family elders on behalf of the couple.

## 3. Sequence of Ritual Steps
1. Sankalpa at home temple with the first 5 printed invitation cards.
2. Presentation at Jagannath Temple / Kuladevata shrine.
3. Distribution of Mahaprasad / Nirmalya to immediate family elders.
