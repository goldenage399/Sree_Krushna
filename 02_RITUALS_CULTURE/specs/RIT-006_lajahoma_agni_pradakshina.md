# 🕉️ RIT-006: Lajahoma & Agni Pradakshina (Sacred Fire Circumambulation)

## 1. Liturgical Meaning & Significance
The brother of the bride (*Kanya Bhrata*) brings puffed paddy/rice (*Khai / Laja*) in a traditional winnowing bamboo basket (*Kula*). The bride, supported by the groom, pours the Laja into the sacred Agni as an oblation, praying for the longevity and prosperity of the husband and household. The couple then circumambulates the sacred Agni (*Agni Pradakshina / Parikrama*).

## 2. Sequence of Ritual Steps
1. **Laja Offering (Puffed Rice):** Brother hands Khai into bride's palms; groom supports her hands as they offer into the Homa Kund.
2. **Agni Pradakshina:** The couple circumambulates the sacred fire four/seven times in clockwise devotion.
3. **Ashmarohana (Treading the Sacred Stone):** The groom leads the bride to tread on a grinding stone (*Silaputa*), symbolizing steadfastness like a rock in the face of life's trials.
