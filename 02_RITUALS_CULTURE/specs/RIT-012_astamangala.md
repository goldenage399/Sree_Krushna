# 🕉️ RIT-012: Astamangala (Eighth-Day Visit to Bride's Parental Home)

## 1. Cultural Meaning & Significance
On the 8th day post-wedding, the newlywed couple visits the bride's parental home for the first time after marriage. The bride's family hosts a lavish traditional feast (*Bhoji*) featuring all favorite dishes of the son-in-law (*Jwai*). Customary gift exchanges (clothes, sweets, silver) and blessings from maternal relatives conclude the initial wedding ceremonies cycle.

## 2. Sequence of Ritual Steps
1. **Arrival & Welcoming:** Groom and bride welcomed with aarti and sweets at bride's maternal home.
2. **Grand Traditional Luncheon / Feast:** Multi-course authentic Odia meal prepared by family.
3. **Gift & Blessing Exchanges:** Presentation of new garments and blessings to the son-in-law.
4. **Return Departure:** Evening return to marital home.
