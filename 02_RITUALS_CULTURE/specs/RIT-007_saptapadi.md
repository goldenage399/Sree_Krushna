# 🕉️ RIT-007: Saptapadi (The Seven Sacred Vows)

## 1. Liturgical Meaning & Significance
Saptapadi constitutes the core legal and spiritual culmination of a Vedic Hindu marriage. By taking seven sacred steps together around the consecrated fire, the bride and groom seal their eternal companionship (*Sakha Saptapada Bhava*). In Odia tradition, seven small heaps of rice/betel nuts or bronze plates (*Kansa Thali*) are stepped upon with sacred Vedic mantras.

## 2. The Seven Sacred Injunctions
1. **Step 1 (Nourishment & Health):** Mutual commitment to provide pure nourishment and sustain bodily health.
2. **Step 2 (Strength & Protection):** Courage and strength to protect each other through adversity.
3. **Step 3 (Prosperity & Wealth):** Righteous accumulation of wealth through dharma (*Dharmatha-Kama*).
4. **Step 4 (Harmony & Happiness):** Mutual respect, joy, and peace in family life.
5. **Step 5 (Progeny & Noble Lineage):** Raising virtuous, educated, and healthy children.
6. **Step 6 (Seasons & Longevity):** Companionship through all seasons of life and joy in every phase.
7. **Step 7 (Lifelong Friendship & Spiritual Union):** Lifelong devotion, spiritual union, and eternal friendship.
