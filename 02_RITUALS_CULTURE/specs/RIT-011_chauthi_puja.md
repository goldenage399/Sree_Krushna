# 🕉️ RIT-011: Chauthi Puja & Sacred Marital Union

## 1. Liturgical Meaning & Significance
*Chauthi* is observed on the fourth night after the wedding day. In Vedic tradition, during the first three nights following marriage, the couple observes spiritual celibacy (*Brahmacharya*). On the fourth night, the family priest conducts a sacred *Chauthi Homa / Puja* invoking Lord Surya, Agni, and Prajapati. Post-puja, the bridal bedchamber (traditionally decorated with fragrant tuberoses, jasmine, and fresh flowers) is consecrated, marking the sacred physical consummation of the marriage.

## 2. Sequence of Ritual Steps
1. **Evening Chauthi Homa:** Priest conducts sacred homa and chants Vedic mantras sanctifying the couple's lifelong companionship.
2. **Khanda Bandha / Knot Untying:** Untying of the sacred cloth knot (*Hastaganthi*) tied during Kanyadaan.
3. **Offering of Scented Milk & Sweets:** Bride brings saffron/almond milk (*Kesara Khira*) to the groom.
4. **Bedchamber Entry (*Phula Sajya*):** Consecrated room entry.
