# 🕉️ RIT-003: Mangan, Mangalakrutya & Haldi Snana

## 1. Liturgical Meaning & Significance
*Mangan* is an ancient Odia rite performed on the eve or dawn of the wedding day. Seven married women (*Sadhaba*) prepare sacred turmeric paste (*Kacha Haladi*), apply auspicious oil and paste to the bride/groom, and conduct the sanctifying bath (*Snana*). The maternal uncle (*Mamu*) plays a revered role in gifting the auspicious yellow attire and bangles.

## 2. Sequence of Ritual Steps
1. **Mangalakrutya & Sankalpa:** Priest invokes the blessings of Kuladevi and family ancestors (*Nandi Mukha Shraddha* if applicable).
2. **Haladi Grinding & Application:** Married women apply turmeric paste with grass blades (*Dubaghasa*).
3. **Snana (Auspicious Bath):** Bathing with consecrated water drawn in sacred pots (*Mangala Ghata*).
4. **Attire Change:** Wearing consecrated yellow attire gifted by maternal relatives.
