# 🕉️ RIT-009: Kanyavida (Bahaghara Vida / The Farewell)

## 1. Cultural Meaning & Significance
*Kanyavida* is the deeply emotional departure of the bride from her maternal childhood home to her husband's home. The bride traditionally takes handfuls of raw rice (*Chaula*) and throws them backward over her head into her mother's outstretched pallu / hands, symbolically repaying the boundless debt of maternal care and praying that prosperity forever fills her parent's household.

## 2. Sequence of Ritual Steps
1. **Rice Throwing (*Chaula Phingiba*):** Bride tosses rice 3 times over her shoulder into her mother's cloth.
2. **Touching Feet & Elder Hugs:** Bride touches the feet of parents, maternal uncles, aunts, and elder relatives.
3. **Escort to Bridal Vehicle:** Escorted by brothers and father to the decorated bridal car [`VEH-01`](file:///d:/GitHub_Repo/Sree_Krushna/05_OPERATIONS_LOGISTICS/transport_and_fleet/).
