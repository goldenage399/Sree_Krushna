# 🕉️ RIT-010: Grihapravesh (Arrival at Groom's Home)

## 1. Cultural Meaning & Significance
The bride arrives at her new marital home and is greeted as the living incarnation of Goddess Lakshmi (*Gruhalakshmi*). Her mother-in-law welcomes her at the threshold with aarti, consecrated milk and water, and blessings.

## 2. Sequence of Ritual Steps
1. **Threshold Aarti:** Welcoming the newlywed couple at the main entrance doorway.
2. **Kula / Mana Overturning:** The bride gently kicks a small measuring vessel (*Mana*) filled with raw rice and coins with her right foot, spilling rice inside the house to bring perennial abundance.
3. **Alta Footprints (*Alata Pada*):** The bride steps into a shallow bronze plate of red *Alta* water and walks upon a long white cloth, leaving auspicious red footsteps leading into the puja room.
4. **Ring Finding & Fun Games (*Kauri / Mundi Khela*):** Traditional playful games where the couple finds a ring hidden in a vessel of milk/colored water, breaking the ice with in-laws.
