# 🕉️ RIT-001: Nirbandha & Ashirbad (Formal Engagement)

## 1. Liturgical Meaning & Significance
Nirbandha is the formal Vedic commitment and promise of alliance (*Vak-Daan*) between the two families in the presence of elders, relatives, and family purohits. The horoscopes (*Jatakas*) and auspicious dates (*Lagna Patrika*) are traditionally confirmed, followed by sacred chanting, ring exchange, and mutual gifting of attire and sweets.

## 2. Timing & Muhurat Window
- **Auspicious Muhurat Window:** `HH:MM` to `HH:MM`
- **Location:** [`VEN-001`](file:///d:/GitHub_Repo/Sree_Krushna/05_OPERATIONS_LOGISTICS/venues/)
- **Officiating Priests:** Bride family priest & Groom family priest

## 3. Sequence of Ritual Steps
1. **Sankalpa & Ganapati Puja:** Invocation of Lord Ganesha for unobstructed proceedings.
2. **Lagna Patrika & Horoscope Vachana:** Reading of the formal marriage alliance document.
3. **Ring & Gift Exchange:** Exchange of rings between bride and groom, and customary clothes (*Ashirbad Pata*).
4. **Elder Blessings (*Ashirbad*):** Elders shower consecrated raw rice (*Arua Chaula*) and grass (*Durva/Dubaghasa*).
5. **Prasad & Sweets Distribution:** Customary sharing of Odia sweets (Rasagola, Chhena Gaja).
