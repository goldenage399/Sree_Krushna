# 🕉️ RIT-008: Sindoor Daan & Mangalsutra Dharan

## 1. Liturgical Meaning & Significance
The groom applies sacred red vermilion (*Sindoor*) using a silver ring/coin or betel leaf into the parting of the bride's hair (*Simanta*), accompanied by Vedic benedictions (*Sumangaliriyam Vadhu*). This marks the bride's formal status as *Saubhagyavati* (the embodiment of auspicious fortune). The sacred necklace (*Mangalsutra*) is fastened, followed by the exchange of flower garlands.

## 2. Sequence of Ritual Steps
1. Consecration of the Sindoor container (*Sindoor Phiri / Karandie*).
2. Groom applies Sindoor from the forehead parting backwards.
3. Fastening of Mangalsutra around bride's neck.
4. Consecrated sweet milk / banana prasadam offered to the couple.
