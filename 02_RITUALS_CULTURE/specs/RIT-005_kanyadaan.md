# 🕉️ RIT-005: Kanyadaan & Hastaganthi

## 1. Liturgical Meaning & Significance
Kanyadaan is the supreme Vedic gift of the daughter by her parents to the groom, invoking divine witnessing from the Pancha Bhutas (Five Elements) and Agni Deva. The hands of the bride and groom are sanctified with sacred Kusha grass, coconut, flowers, and betel leaves, and ritually joined (*Hastaganthi / Panigrahana*), symbolising eternal union and shared dharma, artha, kama, and moksha.

## 2. Sequence of Ritual Steps
1. **Gotra & Pravara Recitation:** Priests chant the 3-generation ancestral lineages of bride and groom.
2. **Kanyadaan Sankalpa:** The Kanyadata (Father/Mother) pours sacred consecrated water into the groom's palms over the bride's hand.
3. **Hastaganthi (Sacred Knot):** The priest ties the sacred cloth knot linking groom's Uttariya and bride's saree pallu with consecrated mango leaves and coconut.
4. **Godana & Dakshina:** Offering of customary charity and dakshina by Kanyadata to the purohits.
