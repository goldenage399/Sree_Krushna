## 2. Experiential Proposals Under Evaluation

### Proposal 1: Haldi Outdoor Rain Dance & Splash Deck with 3-Stage Dry Air-Lock
- **Classification**: `[OPS-LOG]` Civil & Drainage / `[SDCA]` Haldi Exterior Experience
- **Target Event**: Haldi Ceremony (`EVT-003`)
- **Concept**:
  - A 20 ft × 25 ft outdoor rain dance deck situated strictly **outside** the northwest corner of the Haldi Annex.
  - Overhead 2-inch aluminum shower truss fitted with 8 warm-mist nozzles and IP65 waterproof amber wash uplights.
  - Connected to the indoor Haldi Annex via a **3-stage dry transition air-lock** (rubber grid tiles $\rightarrow$ coir drying station $\rightarrow$ absorbent terrycloth runner) to prevent water and yellow turmeric tracks from reaching interior carpeting.
- **Feasibility & Engineering Stress-Test**:
  - **Civil Elevation**: Deck must be elevated 6 inches on steel/composite stringers with a 1:40 outward drainage slope over compacted gravel.
  - **Sullage French Drain**: Perimeter slotted PVC pipe connected to a 0.5 HP automatic submersible pump discharging 30m away.
  - **Electrical Safety**: Dedicated RCD/GFCI breaker on Bus A; zero 220V cables on ground level; all overhead lights 24V low-voltage.
- **Pending Decision Questions for Decorator / Host**:
  - [ ] Does the host wish to invest in an active rain dance deck, or keep Haldi as a traditional seated floral ceremony?
  - [ ] What is the rental cost quoted by the vendor for composite decking vs. basic waterproof tarpaulin?
  - [ ] Where is the nearest pressurized fresh water line for the overhead shower sprayers?
