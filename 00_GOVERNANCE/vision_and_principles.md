# 🌟 Marriage Vision, Values & Decision Principles

**SSOT Scope:** Guiding aesthetic, spiritual, familial, scale, and financial boundaries.

---

## 1. Core Vision Statement

*(To be filled by the Couple & Family)*

- **Spiritual & Cultural Focus:** Traditional Odia Hindu Brahmin rituals performed with Vedic sanctity and precision.
- **Atmosphere & Experience:** Warm, hospitable, dignified, family-centered, and stress-free for all guests and elders.
- **Scale:** *(e.g., Intimate family rituals for pre-wedding; grand reception for broader community).*

---

## 2. Non-Negotiables & Guiding Principles

1. **Ritual Authenticity:** Vedic rituals (Muhurat, Gotra observance, Kanyadaan, Saptapadi, Chauthi) take precedence over stage entertainment.
2. **Elder & Guest Comfort:** Priority on comfortable seating, climate control, accessible transport, and prompt hospitality.
3. **Financial Discipline:** Transparent accounting with pre-approved contracts (`CTR-###`) and clear tracking of cash envelopes for priest dakshina and vendor tips.
4. **Zero Confusion Day-of Operations:** No ambiguity regarding who holds luggage keys, who coordinates caterers, or who escorts VIPs.

---

## 3. Things We Will NOT Do (Anti-Goals)

- ❌ Unplanned last-minute major expense additions without explicit `DEC-###` approval.
- ❌ Fragmented communication across informal chats where agreements get lost.
- ❌ Compromising on priest/purohit requirements and sacred samagri quality.

---

## 4. Decision Governance Protocol

- **Level 1 (Minor < ₹10,000):** Operational coordinator may decide and log task.
- **Level 2 (Major ₹10,000 - ₹1,00,000):** Requires formal `DEC-###` with bride & groom sign-off.
- **Level 3 (Strategic > ₹1,00,000 or Date/Venue Change):** Requires joint family council consensus and freezing in `00_GOVERNANCE/decisions/`.
