# 🏁 Post-Wedding Closure & Archival Protocol

This directory houses the formal handover, return, reconciliation, and post-wedding closure workflows.

---

## Master Closure Checklist

### 1. Financial & Commercial Settlement
- [ ] Final payment release to Caterer (`VDR-###`) post plate-count reconciliation.
- [ ] Final payment release to Venue (`VDR-###`) and security deposit refund receipt.
- [ ] Final payment release to Decorator & Sound/Lighting teams.
- [ ] Priest Dakshina & Temple donation receipts cataloged.
- [ ] Full reconciliation of planned vs actual expenses in `06_FINANCE_COMMERCIALS/budget_master.md`.

### 2. Assets, Rentals & Inventories
- [ ] Return of rented sherwani/attire/accessories with deposit retrieval.
- [ ] Safe storage and locker transfer of bride & groom jewellery.
- [ ] Inventory and cataloging of received family gifts/shagun (`06_FINANCE_COMMERCIALS/gifts_and_shagun/`).
- [ ] Return of hired vehicles and hotel key handovers.

### 3. Media & Deliverables Pipeline
- [ ] Receive raw photo & video drive backup from Photographer (`VDR-###`).
- [ ] Selection and approval of teaser & cinematic wedding highlight video.
- [ ] Selection of album prints and final album delivery.
- [ ] Ingestion into `07_DOCUMENTS_ARCHIVE/digital_media_catalog.md`.

### 4. Statutory, Legal & Social
- [ ] Submission of marriage registration application at Registrar Office (`07_DOCUMENTS_ARCHIVE/legal/`).
- [ ] Receipt of Government Marriage Certificate.
- [ ] Distribution of formal Thank You messages/calls to key guests & family elders.
