# Decision Record: DEC-002

## 1. Context & Business Need
The host is undertaking an on-site physical inspection of Rayagada Convention & Hotel (`VEN-001`), owned by an acquaintance proprietor, to determine its operational capacity to host 35 guest rooms and wedding ceremonies (`EVT-001`). A tangible, print-ready field checklist was required immediately to print at the hotel reception desk and execute a room-by-room audit today.

## 2. Options Evaluated
1. **Option A (2-Page Room Tally Sheet Only):** Ultra-condensed room checklist. Rejected as standalone because it omitted MEP/DG and contractual sign-off.
2. **Option B (4-Page Technical & Commercial Audit Packet):** Formal multi-page dossier. Too academic if not structured for physical clipboard use.
3. **Option C (Unified Modular 4-Page Field Dossier):** Approved. Combines:
   - Part 1 (Pages 1 & 2): 35-Room Inspection Matrix with 10 zero-tolerance parameters.
   - Part 2 (Page 3): MEP & Utility Infrastructure, DG Load Test, and Linen PAR Capacity.
   - Part 3 (Page 4): Management Interview, Capex vs. Opex Rectification Order, Bi-Weekly Milestone Schedule, and Binding Sign-off Block.

## 3. Architecture Council Certification
- **Standard**: Follows Council Deliberation Protocol v1.0 and Reality-First Grounding (RFG-001).
- **Physical Print Target**: Optimized for standard monochrome A4 laser printers available at hotel reception desks (`@page { size: A4 portrait; margin: 8mm; }`).
- **Legal & Commercial Binding**: Demarcates 100% Hotel Capex (owner repairs) vs. Host Opex and establishes an immutable Drop-Dead Date (T-30 Days) for fallback room reallocation.
