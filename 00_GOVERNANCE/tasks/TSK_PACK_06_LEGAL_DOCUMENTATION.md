## Pack Overview
This pack tracks the legally mandatory paperwork that the ceremonial rituals (`RIT-###`) do not cover on their own — marriage registration, ID/witness compliance, venue permits, and event insurance. None of this existed as a tracked entity before this pack.

---

### `TSK-601`: Marriage Registration (Hindu Marriage Act / Special Marriage Act)
* **WBS Code:** `1.5.1`
* **Phase:** Phase 6 (Day +1 to Day +30)
* **Lead Owner:** Sree & Krushna
* **Priority:** Critical
* **Status:** Planned
* **Linked Entities:** `PER-001`, `PER-002`, `EVT-004`

#### Deliverable Description:
Collect ID/age/address proofs for both parties, book the sub-registrar appointment, arrange 2 witnesses, and file for the marriage certificate after `RIT-007` (Saptapadi) is complete.

#### Verification Checklist:
- [ ] Aadhaar, PAN, birth/age proof, and passport-size photos ready for both parties.
- [ ] 2 witnesses (with ID proof) confirmed and briefed on registrar appointment date.
- [ ] Sub-registrar / Tahsildar office appointment booked (online or in-person).
- [ ] Marriage certificate application filed within statutory window post-ceremony.
- [ ] Certified copy of marriage certificate collected and archived.
