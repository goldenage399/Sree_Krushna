## Pack Overview
`TSK-301` tracks the guest *register*; `TSK-302` tracks the physical invitation box. Neither covers the digital layer guests actually use day-to-day to RSVP, get directions, and stay updated. This pack closes that gap.

---

### `TSK-801`: Wedding Website & RSVP Portal
* **WBS Code:** `5.6.1`
* **Phase:** Phase 2 (T-90)
* **Lead Owner:** Sree & Krushna
* **Priority:** High
* **Status:** Planned
* **Linked Entities:** `03_PEOPLE_GUESTS/directory/`

#### Deliverable Description:
Stand up a single wedding website/microsite with event schedule, venue maps, dress code, hotel booking links, and a digital RSVP form that writes back into the master guest register (`PER-###`).

#### Verification Checklist:
- [ ] Website live with schedule, venue, and dress-code info for all `EVT-###`.
- [ ] RSVP form captures guest count, dietary preference, and arrival date.
- [ ] RSVP submissions reconciled weekly against `TSK-301` guest register.
- [ ] Mobile-responsive check done (majority of guests will open on phone).
