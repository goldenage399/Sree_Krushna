# /idea-incubator Workflow

> **Governing Skill:** [idea-incubator SKILL.md](file:///d:/GitHub_Repo/Sree_Krushna/.agent/skills/idea-incubator/SKILL.md)  
> **Output Target:** `docs/proposals/` (`PROP-YYYYMMDD-ideation-intake.md`)  
> **Prime Constraint:** Strictly **Incubation Mode (Exploratory)**. No production code or live cockpit mutations allowed.

---

## Lifecycle Steps

### Step 1: Firewall Lock & Isolation
- Acknowledge that the concept is accepted into the **Incubation Chamber**.
- Explicitly assure the user that this brainstorm session is decoupled from immediate application requirements and no code will be edited.

### Step 2: Read Governing Skill
- Read `.agent/skills/idea-incubator/SKILL.md` to ensure all feasibility criteria and tone guardrails are active.

### Step 3: Experiential & Technical Stress-Test
- Deconstruct the concept into sensory cues, target event, and emotional impact.
- Stress-test physical limits, electrical loads, safety risks (slipping, tripping, fire), and camera/lighting thresholds.
- Formulate concrete, non-jargon questions for the specific vendor (Decorator, DJ/AV, Caterer).

### Step 4: Creative Amplification
- Brainstorm 2–3 low-cost (< ₹5,000) or zero-cost touches (scents, personal voice notes, family participation, lighting cues).

### Step 5: Structured Proposal Logging
- Append or update the concept in the active proposal file in `docs/proposals/PROP-YYYYMMDD-ideation-intake.md` with:
  - Unique Proposal ID (`PROP-###`)
  - Classification (`[SDCA]`, `[OPS-LOG]`, `[GOV-DEC]`, `[APP-ENH]`)
  - Concept & Moment
  - Feasibility & Vendor Questions
  - Amplification Ideas & Risk Mitigations

### Step 6: Hold in Incubation
- Keep the proposal status as `DRAFT`.
- Do NOT proceed to implementation, PRD scaffolding, or cockpit compilation until the host explicitly requests promotion.
