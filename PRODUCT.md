# Sree Krushna Marriage OS — Product Specification

## Product Overview
**Sree Krushna Marriage OS** is an executive digital command tower and operational operating system for the wedding of Sree and Krushna. It unifies high-ceremonial Odia Hindu Vedic liturgies, minute-by-minute multi-track run sheets, asset custody chains, financial visibility, vendor contracts, and collaborative couple planning into a private, authenticated Single-Page Application (SPA).

## Core Personas & Access Tiers
1. **Tier 1 (Core Couple — Sree & Krushna):** SuperAdmins with unrestricted authority over private budgets, gold vaults, vision canvases, and milestone approvals.
2. **Tier 2 (Parents Council & Leads):** Overseers of liturgical traditions, family hospitality, vendor selections, and master guest registers.
3. **Tier 3 (Day Functional Coordinators):** Executors of live day-of run sheets across 6 parallel tracks (Bride, Groom, Priest, Catering, Photography, Fleet). Zero financial visibility.
4. **Tier 4 (Guests & Extended Family):** Viewers of consecrated itineraries, arrival logistics, room blocks, and venue directions.

## Key Capabilities
- **Live Command Center:** Live countdown ticker, milestone progression radar, and 4-tier security overview.
- **6-Track Synchronized Swimlanes:** Parallel tracks with 4 strict operational gate checkpoints (`GATE-01` to `GATE-04`).
- **Real-Time Task Execution (CRUD):** Domain-categorized action items with priority sorting, owner assignment, and persistent status tracking.
- **Vedic Liturgical Encyclopedia:** Complete specifications for 12 Odia Brahmin rites (`RIT-001`..`012`) with itemized samagri checklists (`SAM-001`..`006`).
- **Couple Vision Studio:** Aesthetic canvas with color palettes and cinematography shot wishlists.
- **Vendor Procurement Ledger:** Real-time tracking of contracts, advance payments, SLAs, and coordinator contacts.
- **Gold & Asset Custody (`AST-###`):** Chain-of-custody tracking for precious jewellery with state verification (Bank Vault $\rightarrow$ Box Ready $\rightarrow$ Mandap Handover).
