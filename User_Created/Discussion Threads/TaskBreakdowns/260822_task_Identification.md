# Master Wedding Operations Blueprint

This blueprint treats the wedding as five linked operational projects: **Rayagada Nirbandha**, Odisha pre-wedding production, BBSR wedding rites, BBSR reception, and post-wedding rites/legal registration. Ritual names, sequence, and materials must be finally confirmed by the appointed Odia Brahmin Purohit because regional family practice and the couple’s _gotra, pravara, kula-devata,_ and selected _lagna_ can alter the exact procedure.

Two important controls should be established immediately:

- **Ritual authority:** the Chief Purohit issues the final _vidhi-patra_ and samagri list; the event team must not improvise liturgical details.
- **Legal authority:** Hindu Marriage Act registration and Special Marriage Act solemnisation/registration are different routes. Odisha’s Special Marriage Act process generally involves a 30-day notice period, while the state’s SUJOG marriage-registration workflow asks for identity, residence, invitation, joint photograph, and joint affidavit documents. Confirm the applicable route and current requirements with the concerned Marriage Officer before fixing the post-wedding date. [rtiodisha.gov](https://rtiodisha.gov.in/Pages/printAllManual/office_id:3271/lang:)

For food operations, use a written food-safety plan, potable water, trained food handlers, temperature logs, waste segregation, and separate clean/dirty flows. These controls are consistent with FSSAI’s catering guidance. [fssai.gov](<https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Guidance_Document_Catering_Sector_19_01_2018(4).pdf>)

#### Sacred mandap control

- Keep the mandap free of unnecessary crew.
- Assign one ritual coordinator to call every step.
- Keep one camera on the couple and one on the Purohit/hands.
- Record clean audio separately from the Purohit lapel feed.
- Use no smoke-producing décor or aerosol near the sacred fire.
- Keep fire extinguishers and a fire blanket accessible but visually discreet.
- Do not allow the photographer to interrupt _Sindoor Daan_ or _Hastaganthi_.
- Maintain a written “do not disturb” window around each irreversible rite.

## Section 5: Rayagada–Bhubaneswar Logistics and Fleet Architecture

### Operating model

Use a central **Guest Mobility Control Desk** with four sub-desk functions:

- Arrival desk.
- Hotel desk.
- Venue shuttle desk.
- Emergency and late-arrival desk.

Every vehicle must have a unique call sign such as `RG-01`, `BBSR-ELDER-02`, or `AIRPORT-VIP-03`.

### Arrival architecture

#### Bhubaneswar Airport

- Pre-arrival:
  - Obtain flight number and terminal details.
  - Assign vehicle and driver.
  - Prepare name placard.
  - Share driver contact through WhatsApp/SMS.
- Arrival:
  - Driver waits in the authorised pickup zone.
  - Hospitality volunteer monitors flight status.
  - Elderly or VIP guests receive baggage assistance.
  - Guests receive welcome water, contact card, and hotel direction.
- Escalation:
  - Flight delay under 2 hours: driver remains assigned.
  - Delay above 2 hours: rotate driver if legally and operationally necessary.
  - Lost baggage: hospitality volunteer stays with guest while another vehicle proceeds.
  - Missed connection: move guest to the late-arrival desk.

#### Rayagada Railway Station

- Create train-wise manifests.
- Assign one coordinator per arrival wave.
- Position buses and SUVs before the train arrives.
- Use large bilingual placards.
- Give priority boarding to elderly guests.
- Tag luggage by family grouping.
- Count passengers before leaving the station.
- Reconcile again at hotel arrival.
- Keep one emergency vehicle at the hotel rather than deploying every vehicle to the station.

4. Use the correct extinguisher only if trained.
5. Call venue fire response and emergency services.
6. Do not resume the rite until the Purohit and venue safety lead confirm safety.

#### Medical emergency

1. Call the medical provider and ambulance.
2. Clear a route.
3. Assign one family escort.
4. Protect the guest’s privacy.
5. Record medication/allergy information only with consent.
6. Notify the Wedding Director after care is underway.

#### Missing jewellery or cash

1. Freeze movement of trunks and envelopes.
2. Preserve access logs and CCTV.
3. Recount with the same two-person team.
4. Notify the family security lead.
5. Record facts, not accusations.
6. Escalate to police or insurer where appropriate.

#### Ritual timing crisis

1. Notify the Chief Purohit immediately.
2. Stop speeches, food announcements, and entertainment.
3. Move the couple to the designated holding position.
4. Let the Purohit decide whether any permissible sequence adjustment exists.
5. Keep media silent and out of the mandap.
6. Resume hospitality only after the sacred sequence is protected.

#### Power failure

1. Confirm whether critical circuits are on backup.
2. Preserve mandap lighting, audio, medical, and food safety systems.
3. Isolate the failed circuit.
4. Do not overload the generator with ad hoc connections.
5. Communicate only through the Wedding Director.
6. Record the fault for post-event review.

### Zero-defect closeout

Before declaring the wedding complete, obtain sign-off from:

- Chief Purohit: rites and sacred materials.
- Couple: personal items and media expectations.
- Both parents: jewellery, cash, gifts, and family distributions.
- Food Lead: catering closure and incident log.
- Fleet Lead: guest departures and vehicle reconciliation.
- Media Lead: backups and deliverable acceptance.
- Venue: damage and handover report.
- Finance Lead: invoices, deposits, and pending retention.
- Legal coordinator: registration acknowledgement and document status.

This combination of ritual authority, venue engineering, guest logistics, food safety, security custody, and documentary evidence is what converts a large traditional wedding from a collection of ceremonies into a controlled, auditable event operation.
