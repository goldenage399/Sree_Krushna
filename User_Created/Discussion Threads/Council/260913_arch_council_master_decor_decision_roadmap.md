## 1. Context & Problem Statement

In wedding decor negotiations, decorators frequently attempt to jump straight into aesthetic choices (floral types, stage arches, drapery swatches) before foundational engineering, spatial zoning, and thermodynamic constraints are established. This out-of-sequence discussion creates fragmented decor, inflated costs, and severe operational regressions on event day.

Following the detailed operational analysis of lines 3370–4330 in `User_Created/Discussion Threads/DecoratorDiscussion/260913_DecoratorDiscussion.md`, the executive team directed that all 20 decision steps across the 24 planning phases be structured into a rigorous **precedence ladder** and rendered interactively inside the **Decorator Negotiation Cockpit**.

---

## 2. The 3-Tier Evaluation & Precedence Matrix

The Councils evaluated three implementation strategies:
1. **Option 1 (Static SSOT Markdown Only)**: Authoring a comprehensive reference document in the repo. *Verdict*: Necessary as the durable source of truth, but passive during live vendor discussions.
2. **Option 2 (Cockpit HUD Text Hotfix)**: Squeezing a checklist into the existing HUD pane. *Verdict*: Cluttered, violates UI craft principles (`--sidebar-width: 340px`), and destroys information hierarchy.
3. **Option 3 (Certified Hybrid — 3-Surface Master Decision Engine)**: Establish a canonical SSOT specification, mirror it for vendor collaboration, and deploy a dedicated **"🏛️ Master Decision Center"** view in the Cockpit with progress tracking, status filters, industry benchmarks, and `localStorage` persistence. *Verdict*: **Unanimously Approved.**

## 3. Architecture Council Certified Ruling (`AC-DEC-2026-010` / `UI-DEC-2026-006`)

### 3.1 The 20-Step Master Precedence Sequence
Decisions must be resolved strictly in the following 5-phase order:

#### Phase I: Site & Spatial Foundations (Steps 1–5)
- **Step 01: Function Schedule & Timings [LOCKED 🟢]**: Fixed by `AC-DEC-2026-007` (Haldi Day 1 morning, Mehendi split, Sangeet Day 1 night, Wedding Day 2 morning).
- **Step 02: Physical Site Survey & Boundary Clearances [VENDOR ACTION 🔵]**: 50m covered corridor from hotel side porch, soil compaction, 6m emergency fire lane perimeter.
- **Step 03: Sun Path, Weather & Thermodynamic Orientation [LOCKED 🟢]**: Gable ends North-South; afternoon sun shielded behind rear BOH kitchen.
- **Step 04: Master Spatial Zoning (Zones A–E) [LOCKED 🟢]**: Defined in `open_ground_spatial_zoning.md`.
- **Step 05: Guest vs Service Circulation Separation [VENDOR ACTION 🔵]**: 100% strict bifurcation; zero staff trolleys through guest arrival foyer.

#### Phase II: Structural Shell & Climate Engineering (Steps 6–7)
- **Step 06: Marquee Structure Architecture [LOCKED 🟢]**: 120ft × 80ft German clear-span hangar profile (6061-T6 aluminum, 14ft eaves, 24ft apex, 100 km/h wind rating).
- **Step 07: Dual-Zone HVAC & DG Power Backbone [LOCKED 🟢]**: 40T package AC in Zone A, desert evaporative coolers in Zone C, dual 125 kVA DGs on a 3-bus SLD with online UPS.

#### Phase III: Design Language & Material Curation (Steps 8–10)
- **Step 08: Master Color Palette & Metallic Standard [PENDING DECISION 🟡]**:
  - *Primary*: Ivory, Warm White, Champagne.
  - *Secondary*: Soft Beige, Muted Gold.
  - *Dominant Metallic*: **Champagne Gold (brushed satin)** &mdash; strictly prohibiting bright yellow/chrome brass clashes.
  - *Action*: Family to select accent tone (Royal Amber vs Blush Rose vs Emerald).
- **Step 09: Material Discipline & Prohibited Negative List [PENDING SIGN-OFF 🟡]**:
  - *Allowed*: Natural teak, satin, brushed brass, fresh seasonal florals, raw silk, cane/rattan.
  - *Prohibited*: Cheap printed flex banners, plastic chairs, artificial nylon florals at eye level, exposed wiring.
  - *Penalty*: ₹50,000 per violation clause in `CTR-DECOR-RIDER-001`.
- **Step 10: Permanent Luxury Base Infrastructure [LOCKED 🟢]**: "Build once, transform multi" &mdash; shell, ceiling draping, chandeliers, and neutral carpet remain constant across all 4 events, saving 45–55% of decor budget.

#### Phase IV: Scenography, Florals & Sensory Experience (Steps 11–15)
- **Step 11: Cinema Lighting Design (CRI > 95) [LOCKED 🟢]**: 3200K cinema profile for indoor altar, 5600K for daytime lawn; zero 120fps video banding on Sony FX3 cameras.
- **Step 12: Floral Strategy & Structural Reuse Matrix [PENDING DECISION 🟡]**:
  - High density at Mandap & Entrance; low-medium at Dining.
  - Structural frames reused; surface accent strings swapped during morning turnaround.
  - *Action*: Select between Traditional Vedic Marigold/Mogra vs Modern English Garden Rose vs Hybrid.
- **Step 13: Furniture Language & Seating Typography [PENDING DECISION 🟡]**:
  - Zone A: Champagne Chiavari chairs + front 2 rows royal velvet sofas for immediate elders (PER-001/PER-002/PER-012).
  - Zone D: Low wooden charpai daybeds + Rajasthani parasols.
  - *Action*: Approve chair finish and cushion fabric samples.
- **Step 14: Sacred Mandap Architecture & Smoke Extraction [LOCKED 🟢]**: 24ft clear stage, 18" riser, 2mm galvanized zinc subfloor barrier, suspended 1200 CFM brass/glass negative-pressure cowl.
- **Step 15: Catering Pass & Guest Activity Stalls [PENDING DECISION 🟡]**:
  - Dual 24ft linear buffets + 640 sq. ft. screened BOH pass.
  - *Action*: Select guest activity booths (Lac bangle artisan, Ittar perfume bar, Polaroid memory book, 360 spin booth).

#### Phase V: Transformation, Safety & Final Sign-Off (Steps 16–20)
- **Step 16: 4-Event Transformation Matrix [PENDING DECISION 🟡]**:
  - Mehendi → Sangeet (2.5h transition): Ambient dimming, moving heads activated, Zone A curtain drawn.
  - Sangeet → Wedding (6h overnight transition): Strike DJ console, erect Lotus Mandap dome, swap floral accents to red/white/gold, switch to 3200K temple lighting.
- **Step 17: Photography Sightlines, Drone & Cable Audit [LOCKED 🟢]**: Strictly no drone flights inside marquee; center aisle 8ft clear dolly track; all cables concealed in rubber ramps.
- **Step 18: Commercial BOQ & 4-Tier Budget Settlement [LOCKED 🟢]**: Tier A (₹6.5L Base Shell), Tier B (₹4.8L Transformations), Tier C (₹3.2L Variables). Settlement target ₹11.5L–₹12.5L.
- **Step 19: Technical & Fire Safety Certification [VENDOR ACTION 🔵]**: Fire Marshal clearance, flame-retardant fabric certification, 4× CO2 + 4× ABC extinguishers, DG ground earthing < 2 ohms.
- **Step 20: Physical Mock-up & Material Sample Gate [VENDOR ACTION 🔵]**: 1:1 physical inspection at T-14 days for fabric drapes, champagne gold finish, chair cushions, and smoke cowl mockup.
