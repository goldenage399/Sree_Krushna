## 1. Project Context & Visual Ambition

The Sree Krushna wedding celebration takes place on an expansive flat open ground approximately 50 meters adjacent to the host hotel. To maximize commercial efficiency and visual grandeur, all events—Mehendi, Sangeet, Vedic Vivaha, and Reception—are integrated under a **Permanent Luxury Base Infrastructure (120ft × 80ft German Clear-Span Marquee)** that seamlessly transforms across day and night functions.

This dossier provides the decorator and production design team with:
1. The **official photorealistic reference imagery** approved by the executive family committee.
2. The **exact generative prompts and lighting directives** used to render each plate, allowing your 3D visualizers to tweak colors, materials, and camera angles directly.
3. The **underlying contractual riders and physical dimensions** that must be met during execution.

## 3. How to Tweak These Prompts for 3D Pre-Visualizations

To adapt these prompts for your team's 3D render engine (Midjourney v6, Stable Diffusion, DALL-E, or Unreal Engine):
1. **Changing Day to Night**: Replace `bright natural daylight` with `dramatic twilight blue sky, warm glowing festoon bulbs, golden candle illumination`.
2. **Changing Color Theme**: Swap `marigold yellow and crimson` with your proposed color palette (e.g., `blush pink, ivory champagne, and eucalyptus sage green`).
3. **Changing Floral Density**: Specify `monolithic floral archways` or `minimalist brass geometric frames with asymmetric floral clusters`.
4. **Camera Angles**: Append `wide architectural elevation shot --ar 16:9` for wide stage views, or `close-up detailed macro shot 85mm f/2.0` for table centerpieces.
