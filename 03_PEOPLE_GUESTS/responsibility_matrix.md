# 👥 Master Family & Coordinator Responsibility (RACI) Matrix

**SSOT Scope:** Explicit allocation of responsibilities across family members, coordinators, and leads to eliminate day-of operational ambiguity.

---

## RACI Definitions
- **R (Responsible):** The doer who executes the task.
- **A (Accountable):** The primary owner who has ultimate decision/sign-off authority.
- **C (Consulted):** Key stakeholders consulted before decisions.
- **I (Informed):** People kept updated on progress.
