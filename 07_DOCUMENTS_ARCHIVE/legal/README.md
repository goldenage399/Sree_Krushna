# 📜 Statutory & Legal Documents Directory (`legal/`)

**SSOT Scope:** Master repository of statutory identification copies, affidavits, marriage registration filings, priest certificates, and government registration records.

---

## Legal Checklist & Filings

1. **Government Marriage Registration (Special Marriage Act / Hindu Marriage Act):**
   - Application Form & Joint Affidavits
   - 3x Witness Identifications (`PER-###`)
   - Proof of Age & Address (Aadhaar/Passport scans)
   - Wedding Invitation Card Copy
   - Officiating Priest Signed Marriage Certificate (*Purohit Patra*)
   - Formal Marriage Certificate Issued by Registrar of Marriages
2. **Identity & Travel Documents for Honeymoon / Outstation Travel:**
   - Passport & Visa copies
   - Flight & Hotel booking confirmations
