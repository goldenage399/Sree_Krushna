# 👑 EVT-004: Barat, Kanyadaan & Main Vedic Wedding Ceremony

## 1. Event Overview
The central milestone of the marriage lifecycle: Barat procession arrival, traditional Baranugam welcome, Varamala garland exchange, Vedic Mandap ceremonies (Kanyadaan, Hastaganthi, Lajahoma, Saptapadi, Sindoor Daan), grand traditional dinner feast, and emotional Kanyavida farewell.

## 2. Sequence of Vedic Milestones
1. `06:30 PM` — Barat Procession Assembles & Proceeds to Venue
2. `07:30 PM` — [`RIT-004: Baranugam & Entrance Welcome`](file:///d:/GitHub_Repo/Sree_Krushna/02_RITUALS_CULTURE/specs/RIT-004_baranugam.md)
3. `08:00 PM` — Jay Mala / Varamala Stage Ceremony
4. `08:30 PM` — Dinner Feast Banquet Opens for Guests
5. `09:30 PM` — Mandap Entry & [`RIT-005: Kanyadaan`](file:///d:/GitHub_Repo/Sree_Krushna/02_RITUALS_CULTURE/specs/RIT-005_kanyadaan.md)
6. `10:30 PM` — [`RIT-006: Lajahoma`](file:///d:/GitHub_Repo/Sree_Krushna/02_RITUALS_CULTURE/specs/RIT-006_lajahoma_agni_pradakshina.md) & [`RIT-007: Saptapadi`](file:///d:/GitHub_Repo/Sree_Krushna/02_RITUALS_CULTURE/specs/RIT-007_saptapadi.md)
7. `11:30 PM` — [`RIT-008: Sindoor Daan`](file:///d:/GitHub_Repo/Sree_Krushna/02_RITUALS_CULTURE/specs/RIT-008_sindoor_daan.md)
8. `01:00 AM` — [`RIT-009: Kanyavida (Farewell)`](file:///d:/GitHub_Repo/Sree_Krushna/02_RITUALS_CULTURE/specs/RIT-009_kanyavida.md) & Departure in Bridal Car [`VEH-01`](file:///d:/GitHub_Repo/Sree_Krushna/05_OPERATIONS_LOGISTICS/transport_and_fleet/)
