# 👑 EVT-004: Barat, Kanyadaan & Main Vedic Wedding Ceremony (Daytime Vivaha)

## 1. Event Overview
The central milestone of the marriage lifecycle configured for a **Daytime Vedic Vivaha** with the primary astrological **Hastaganthi Muhurat at 12:00 PM (Noon)**: Morning Barat procession arrival, traditional Baranugam welcome, daylight Varamala garland exchange, Vedic Mandap ceremonies (Kanyadaan, 12:00 PM Hastaganthi, Lajahoma, Saptapadi, Sindoor Daan), grand traditional Odia Vivaha Bhoji afternoon feast, and afternoon Kanyavida farewell.

## 2. Sequence of Vedic Milestones (Daytime Schedule)
1. `09:30 AM` — Barat Procession Assembles & Proceeds to Venue
2. `10:30 AM` — [`RIT-004: Baranugam & Entrance Welcome`](file:///d:/GitHub_Repo/Sree_Krushna/02_RITUALS_CULTURE/specs/RIT-004_baranugam.md) (Aarti, Floral Shower, Morning Refreshment Coolers)
3. `11:00 AM` — Jay Mala / Varamala Stage Ceremony (Daylight Ambiance)
4. `11:30 AM` — Mandap Entry & Preliminary Kanyadaan Rites ([`RIT-005: Kanyadaan`](file:///d:/GitHub_Repo/Sree_Krushna/02_RITUALS_CULTURE/specs/RIT-005_kanyadaan.md))
5. `12:00 PM` — 🪔 **THE SACRED ASTROLOGICAL MUHURAT: HASTAGANTHI** (Holy Knot & Hand-Joining)
6. `12:30 PM` — Grand Traditional Odia Vivaha Bhoji (Afternoon Lunch Feast Opens for Guests)
7. `12:45 PM` — [`RIT-006: Lajahoma`](file:///d:/GitHub_Repo/Sree_Krushna/02_RITUALS_CULTURE/specs/RIT-006_lajahoma_agni_pradakshina.md) & [`RIT-007: Saptapadi`](file:///d:/GitHub_Repo/Sree_Krushna/02_RITUALS_CULTURE/specs/RIT-007_saptapadi.md) (Agni Pradakshina & Seven Vows)

9. `02:30 PM` — Couple Mandap Ashirbad & Family Dining
10. `03:30 PM` — [`RIT-009: Kanyavida (Farewell)`](file:///d:/GitHub_Repo/Sree_Krushna/02_RITUALS_CULTURE/specs/RIT-009_kanyavida.md) & Afternoon Departure in Bridal Car [`VEH-01`](file:///d:/GitHub_Repo/Sree_Krushna/05_OPERATIONS_LOGISTICS/transport_and_fleet/)
