# 🎶 EVT-002: Mehendi & Sangeet Evening

## 1. Event Overview
An informal, joyous celebration featuring bridal henna application, family musical performances, folk/contemporary dance, live dinner stations, and late-night gala.

## 2. Spatial Setup & Dual-Sanctuary Architecture (AC-DEC-2026-007)
- **Phase 1 Venue (12:00 – 14:30):** Hotel AC Hall ([`VEN-001`](file:///d:/GitHub_Repo/Sree_Krushna/05_OPERATIONS_LOGISTICS/venues/VEN-001_rayagada_engagement_venue.md)) — Intimate, climate-controlled bridal henna application (zero humidity/heat).
- **Phase 2 Venue (14:30 – 17:00):** Open-Ground Marquee Zone D ([`VEN-002`](file:///d:/GitHub_Repo/Sree_Krushna/05_OPERATIONS_LOGISTICS/venues/open_ground_spatial_zoning.md)) — Outdoor festive promenade, live chaat stalls, bangle bars, folk musicians.
- **Phase 3 Venue (19:00 – 00:30):** Open-Ground Marquee Zone A & C ([`VEN-002`](file:///d:/GitHub_Repo/Sree_Krushna/05_OPERATIONS_LOGISTICS/venues/open_ground_spatial_zoning.md)) — Grand Sangeet Bollywood concert, P3 LED screen, dance floor, gala buffet.
- **Marquee Technical Window (10:00 – 17:30):** Zone A (Main Stage) is acoustically screened off, providing the technical crew 7.5 hours of uninterrupted rigging time for the 24ft P3 LED screen, moving heads, and audio line-arrays without guest interference.
