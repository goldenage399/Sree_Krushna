# 🌿 EVT-003: Mangan, Mangalakrutya & Haldi Ceremony

## 1. Event Overview
Auspicious morning ceremony where the bride and groom at their respective family residences (or designated venue) undergo the sacred turmeric application (*Mangan*) and sanctifying bath by married women (*Sadhaba*).

1. `08:00 AM` — Mangalakrutya & Ancestral Prayers with Purohit
