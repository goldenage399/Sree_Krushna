# 🏡 EVT-006: Grihapravesh & Chauthi Ceremony

## 1. Event Overview
Customary arrival of the bride at the groom's home (*Grihapravesh*), welcoming her with the rice-pot tipping and red alta footsteps, followed on the 4th night by the sacred Vedic *Chauthi Puja*, untying of the sacred knot (*Hastaganthi*), and entry into the consecrated bedchamber (*Phula Sajya*).

## 2. Program Schedule
1. `10:30 AM` — Arrival at Home & [`RIT-010: Grihapravesh Welcoming Rites`](file:///d:/GitHub_Repo/Sree_Krushna/02_RITUALS_CULTURE/specs/RIT-010_grihapravesh.md)
2. `11:30 AM` — Icebreaker Games (*Kauri Khela*) & Family Introductions
3. `01:00 PM` — Family Feast
4. `07:30 PM` — Evening [`RIT-011: Chauthi Homa & Puja`](file:///d:/GitHub_Repo/Sree_Krushna/02_RITUALS_CULTURE/specs/RIT-011_chauthi_puja.md)
5. `09:30 PM` — Untying of Sacred Knot & Bedchamber Entry (*Phula Sajya*)
