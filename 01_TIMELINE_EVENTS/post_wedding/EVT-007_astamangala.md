# 🌺 EVT-007: Astamangala (Eighth-Day Parental Visit)

## 1. Event Overview
Traditional eighth-day visit by the newlyweds to the bride's maternal home, concluding the wedding cycle with a grand festive lunch hosted in honor of the new son-in-law (*Jwai*) and bride.

## 2. Program Schedule
1. `11:30 AM` — Arrival at Bride's Parental Home & Welcome
2. `01:00 PM` — Grand Traditional Odia Multi-Course Feast ([`RIT-012`](file:///d:/GitHub_Repo/Sree_Krushna/02_RITUALS_CULTURE/specs/RIT-012_astamangala.md))
3. `03:30 PM` — Gift Presentations & Family Blessings
4. `05:00 PM` — Conclusion & Evening Return
