# 🍲 Food, Hospitality & Catering Management

**SSOT Scope:** Master menus, tastings, authentic Odia wedding feast preparations, special dietary accommodations (diabetic/pure vegetarian), and plate-count auditing.

---

## Feast Menus Overview

1. **Pre-Wedding Snacks & High Tea:** Light Odia snacks (Chhena Gaja, Nimki, tea/coffee counters).
2. **Main Wedding Feast (Vedic / Odia Traditional Feast):**
   - Pure ghee rice, Kanika / Pulao
   - Traditional Dalma, Besara, Ghanta Tarkari
   - Chhena Tarkari, Khatta (Oau / Tomato-Khajura)
   - Odia Sweets: Chhena Poda, Rasagola, Rabidi / Payas
3. **Grand Reception Multi-Cuisine Buffet:**
   - Welcome mocktails & live chaat counters
   - North Indian, Continental & Odia specialty entrees
   - Live dessert & ice cream counters
4. **Priest & Fasting Elders Special Meals:** Dedicated Sattvic, onion-garlic free prep.
