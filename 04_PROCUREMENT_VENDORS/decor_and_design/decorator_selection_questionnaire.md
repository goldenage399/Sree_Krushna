## 🎯 1. Mandatory Decorator Evaluation Questionnaire

Ask each shortlisted decorator / event fabrication vendor to provide formal answers to these criteria:

| # | Evaluation Category | Critical Questions for Decorator | Mandatory Standard / Acceptance Criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Fresh Floral Sourcing & Quality** | • What percentage of the stage and entrance flowers are 100% fresh vs artificial?<br>• What are the exact flower varieties used (e.g. Bangalore Marigolds, Kolkata Tuberoses, Dutch Roses, Jasmine)?<br>• When will the flowers be harvested and installed to guarantee zero wilting before the 10:00 AM muhurat? | • 100% fresh flowers for mandap, stage ring, and entrance torana.<br>• Flowers delivered on-site by 04:00 AM on event day. |
| **2** | **Stage & Structural Safety** | • What is the load-bearing capacity of the elevated stage (minimum 25–30 people standing)?<br>• Are all truss structures bolted, counter-weighted, and fire-retardant treated?<br>• Do you provide certified non-slip red/ivory stage carpeting? | • Stage tested for 2.5 metric tons.<br>• No exposed electrical wiring or sharp truss edges. |
| **3** | **Lighting & Mood Ambience** | • What lighting fixtures are included (Warm LED pars, profile follow-spots, warm fairy strings, amber washes)?<br>• Is lighting tuned for cinema 4K cameras (5600K daylight or 3200K warm tungsten, zero flicker at 120fps)?<br>• Will you provide on-site lighting technicians throughout the event? | • Flicker-free cinema grade lighting.<br>• Dedicated lighting board operator during ceremony. |
| **4** | **Power & Generator Contingency** | • Is a silent diesel generator (DG) included in your quote or sourced separately?<br>• Does your electrical distribution box (DB) have RCD/MCB circuit breakers to prevent Mandap power trips? | • Min 40 kVA backup DG with automatic AMF switch. |
| **5** | **Rayagada Local Logistics** | • Have you previously executed wedding decor at venues in Rayagada / South Odisha?<br>• Where will your fabrication and labor crew be accommodated?<br>• By what time on 10th Feb (eve of engagement) will the basic truss and backdrop frame be ready? | • Full framework inspection ready by 20:00 on eve of event.<br>• Final fresh flowers completed by 07:00 AM morning. |
| **6** | **Pricing, Inclusions & SLAs** | • Does the quote include 300 banquet chair covers with gold bows, dining hall tables, and VIP sofa?<br>• What is your payment schedule (e.g. 30% advance, 50% on framework erection, 20% post-event sign-off)? | • Itemized transparent quote; 20% withheld until event conclusion. |
