## 🎨 2. Detailed Decor & Staging Elements

### A. Grand Entrance Gate (*Swagata Torana*)
* **Structure:** 12 ft high × 14 ft wide solid truss arched torana.
* **Florals:** Real marigold garlands (*Gendu Phula*), hanging jasmine strings (*Juhi/Beli*), fresh mango leaves (*Amba Patra*), and traditional brass water urns (*Purna Kumbha*) on carved wooden pedestals.
* **Welcome Board:** Velvet-finish antique gold easel board: *"Welcome to the Engagement Ceremony of Sree & Krushna — 11th February 2027"*.

### B. The Engagement Centerstage (*Nirbandha Mandap*)
* **Dimensions:** 30 feet width × 18 feet depth × 2.5 feet stage height with safety side-steps.
* **Backdrop Architecture:** 
  * Central circular floral ring (8 ft diameter) with dense yellow and orange marigolds, cream roses, and white tuberoses.
  * Backlit 3D warm-gold LED monogram crest: **👑 S & K**.
  * Draped beige and royal ivory silk back curtains with cascading floral fairy lights (2700K warm white).
* **Seating on Stage:** Royal two-seater velvet gold-carved sofa (*Maharaja Chair*) flanked by side armchairs for parents during paternal vow recitation.
* **Vedic Puja Setup on Stage:** Low brass chowki (*Pidha*) with embroidered red velvet cloth for *Patra Paribartana* (horoscope scroll exchange), silver thali, raw rice, sandalwood paste, betel nuts (*Gua*), and coconut.

### C. Photo-Op & Monogram Memory Wall
* **Location:** Near main foyer / dining entrance.
* **Design:** 10 ft × 8 ft botanical foliage green wall accented with pastel roses, hanging brass bells, and warm neon typography: *"Forever Begins Today — Sree & Krushna"*.
* **Lighting:** Dedicated warm spotlights at 45 degree angle for studio-grade smartphone & camera portraits without harsh shadows.

### D. Audio & Electrical Infrastructure
* **Stage Mic:** 2× Wireless hand-held mics + 1 podium mic for Purohit horoscope recitation and family announcements.
* **Sound System:** 4-speaker distributed PA system tuned for clarity (no echo in the hall).
* **Power Contingency:** Dedicated 40 kVA on-site generator with automatic changeover switch (AMF) to ensure zero blackout during the ring exchange.
