# 🌺 Decor, Floral & Visual Spatial Design

**SSOT Scope:** Color palettes, themes, mandap structure specifications, stage layouts, floral requirements, and ambient lighting design.

---

## Visual Design Areas

1. **Mandap Setup:** Traditional Odia Vedic aesthetic with fresh flowers (marigold, tuberose, jasmine), safe havan kund fire/smoke ventilation, and raised platform.
2. **Main Reception Stage:** Elegant backdrop, couple sofa seating, warm portrait lighting, and floral framing.
3. **Entrance & Walkway:** Torana / welcome arch, traditional welcome boards, rangoli / floral pathways.
4. **Dining & Buffet Hall:** Clean buffet line setups, table centerpieces, hydration counters.
5. **Photo Booth / Selfie Zone:** Aesthetic themed backdrop for friends and relatives.
