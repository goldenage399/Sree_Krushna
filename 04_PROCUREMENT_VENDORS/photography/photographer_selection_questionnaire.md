| **6** | **Payment Milestones & Contract Terms** | • What is your milestone schedule (e.g. 25% booking, 50% on event day, 25% on final album approval)?<br>• Do you offer a formal contract with cancellation and backup-crew replacement clauses? | • Never pay 100% upfront; reserve 20–25% for post-delivery album sign-off. |

## 📋 Part 3: Pre-Wedding Shoot Locations & Styling Guide

1. **Location 1: Puri Golden Beach (Blue Flag Area)**
   * **Vibe:** Sunrise coastal elegance, soft pastel waves.
   * **Timing:** 05:30 AM – 08:00 AM (Golden hour morning light).
   * **Attire:** Flowy pastel pink/peach chiffon saree & ivory linen kurta/shirt.
2. **Location 2: Konark Marine Drive Pine Forest Canopy**
   * **Vibe:** Cinematic woodland, dappled tree canopy, bohemian greenery.
   * **Timing:** 09:00 AM – 11:30 AM.
   * **Attire:** Emerald green silk dress & tailored neutral linen.
3. **Location 3: Mukteshvara Temple Sandstone Sculptures (Bhubaneswar)**
   * **Vibe:** 10th-century classical Kalinga temple architecture, heritage sacred alliance.
   * **Timing:** 03:30 PM – 05:30 PM (Warm afternoon sandstone glow).
   * **Attire:** Pure Sambalpuri handloom silk saree & matching Sambalpuri bordered Kurta.
4. **Location 4: Chilika Lake Twilight Waters (Mangalajodi / Rambha)**
   * **Vibe:** Romantic wooden boat reflections, golden sunset backwaters.
   * **Timing:** 05:00 PM – 06:30 PM (Sunset glow).
   * **Attire:** Royal midnight blue evening gown & formal tailored suit.
