## 1. Notice to Shortlisted Decorator Vendors

This binding Tender Rider forms an inseparable schedule to any quotation, commercial work order, or contract executed for venue styling, marquee fabrication, and production.

### Core Tender Mandate:
Vendors are hereby informed that the client is **not soliciting four separate decoration setups**. The client requires **one permanent luxury base environment that intelligently transforms across four consecutive functions** (Haldi $\rightarrow$ Mehendi $\rightarrow$ Sangeet $\rightarrow$ Wedding). 

> *"Ek baar achha luxury base bana do — phir har function mein usi base ko intelligently transform karo. Structure same rahega, look aur mood function ke hisaab se change hoga."*

Any proposal submitting four independent marquee, flooring, or ceiling charges will be **disqualified immediately**.

## 3. The 6 Liquidated Damage & SLA Enforcement Clauses

1. **Turnover Punctuality SLA:**
   - Turnovers between events must strictly adhere to the operational windows:
     - Haldi $\rightarrow$ Mehendi: Completed in **90 minutes** (10:00 to 11:30).
     - Mehendi $\rightarrow$ Sangeet: Completed in **120 minutes** (16:00 to 18:00).
     - Sangeet $\rightarrow$ Wedding Mandap: Completed and ready for joint inspection by **07:00 AM** on wedding day.
   - **Penalty:** Any turnover delay exceeding 30 minutes incurs an automatic liquidated damage deduction of **₹15,000 per hour of delay**, deducted directly from the final retention fund.
2. **100% Fresh Floral Authenticity Guarantee:**
   - Mandap pillars, sacred toranas, and stage ring must use 100% fresh Bangalore marigolds and Kolkata Rajnigandha. Artificial plastic flowers discovered in prime ceremonial zones will result in an immediate **25% forfeiture of the floral line item**.
3. **Flicker-Free 4K Cinema Lighting Guarantee (CRI > 95):**
   - All stage and mandap profile fixtures must deliver minimum 95 CRI, calibrated 3200K tungsten / 5600K daylight, and demonstrate zero banding or flicker at 120fps during the cinematographer calibration test. Non-compliant fixtures must be replaced within 60 minutes or their rental cost will be forfeited.
4. **Autonomous Dual-DG Power Redundancy & 3-Bus SLD:**
   - Power distribution must operate via 2 × 125 kVA Silent DGs with an Automatic Mains Failure (AMF) synchronizing panel, partitioned into Bus A (HVAC), Bus B (Sound/Electronics), and Bus C (Catering/Utility). Any electrical blackout stalling ceremonies for over 60 seconds incurs a **₹20,000 liquidated penalty**.
5. **Sacred Havan Smoke Extraction & Fire Safety:**
   - The vendor must provide and erect a suspended telescopic havan smoke extraction hood connected to an external negative-pressure inline exhaust blower, paired with a 2mm galvanized zinc subfloor plate beneath the copper kund and 4× CO2 fire extinguishers. Any smoke dispersion triggering guest eye-stinging or camera fogging incurs an automatic **₹25,000 liquidated safety deduction**.
6. **18% GST All-Inclusive Commercial Standard:**
   - All rates quoted in this schedule are strictly **100% All-Inclusive of 18% GST, labor cess, generator diesel, transportation, and post-event debris removal**. No subsequent tax surcharges, unloading levies, or fuel price escalation claims shall be accepted under any circumstances.
7. **The 20% Post-Event Retention Gate:**
   - Payment schedule is strictly: **30% Advance on signing**, **50% upon verified physical erection of base marquee and AC commissioning by 20:00 on eve of Day 1**, and **20% strictly retained until post-wedding hall inspection**.
   - Zero claims for >80% disbursement prior to wedding conclusion will be entertained.
8. **Turnkey Labor Self-Sufficiency & Off-Site Lodging Invariant (AC-DEC-2026-007):**
   - The vendor is 100% responsible for the off-site lodging, boarding, sanitary facilities, meals, and local transit of all 15–20 fabrication, electrical, and floral crew members. The client provides **ZERO hotel rooms or guest facilities** for contractor personnel. Any unauthorized crew occupancy in client-booked rooms will be deducted directly from vendor retention at ₹5,000/room/night.
9. **Aerial Drone Flight Safety & Marquee Exclusion (AC-DEC-2026-007):**
   - To protect ceiling fabric linings, crystal chandeliers, floral swags, and the sacred havan fire altar, **zero unmanned aerial vehicles (drones) may be operated inside the enclosed marquee structure**. Drone cinematography is restricted strictly to exterior open grounds, the Barat procession on the roadway, and the hotel facade.
